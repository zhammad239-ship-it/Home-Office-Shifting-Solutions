Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our shifting services.

How do I book Home and Office Shifting Solutions?

Simply message us on WhatsApp at 0346 2280418 or call us. Share your moving details and we'll provide a free quote and confirm your booking.

Which areas in Karachi do you serve?

We serve all of Karachi including New Golimar, Lasbella Garden, Nazimabad, Gulshan, North Nazimabad, Orangi, Liaquatabad, and surrounding areas. Contact us for moves outside Karachi.

Do you provide packing materials?

Yes! We bring all necessary packing materials including bubble wrap, cartons, tape, and protective covers for furniture. Our packing service is complete and professional.

Is my furniture and belongings insured?

We take extreme care with all items. Our team is trained to handle fragile and valuable items safely. Please discuss specific insurance needs with us during booking.

How much does home shifting cost?

Cost depends on distance, quantity of items, and floor level (with/without lift). Contact us on WhatsApp for a free, no-obligation quote tailored to your needs.

How long does a typical move take?

Most residential moves take 4-8 hours depending on size. Office relocations may take longer. We always aim to complete your move efficiently within the same day.
